The usual routine

Tea, sugar and milk travel in separate packets that are easy to lose and messy to open.

Green Sip puts the cup, the tea, the sugar and the milk in one pack. The cup is part of the snack.

    The experience

    From order to last bite.

    STEP 1

    Choose

    Pick your cup flavour, tea bag or coffee bag, and your sugar on this page. That is your Make Your Own Deal.

    STEP 2

    Receive

    Your kit is delivered to your door. It holds the cup, two biscuits and a ready pack of tea or coffee, sugar and milk powder.

    STEP 3

    Add boiling water

    Put your tea bag or coffee bag with the sugar and milk powder in the cup, then just add boiling water.

    STEP 4

    Sip, then eat

    Drink up, then eat the cup. If you added the chocolate spoon, that is dessert too.

    Our story

    A cleaner, greener way to enjoy tea and coffee.

    Our vision

    To become a leading sustainable beverage brand in Pakistan, with edible cups, eco-friendly solutions and smart technology.

    Our mission
    1. Reduce plastic and paper cup waste with tea and coffee in flavoured edible cups.
    2. Offer affordable, customizable drinks, including sugar-free options.
    3. Bring ready-to-use beverage kits and online ordering with smart tools.
    4. Serve Rahim Yar Khan and beyond, with online ordering and home delivery.
